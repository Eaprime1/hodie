## The Plexus — Universal Workshop Architecture

The plexus is the processing pipeline. Every workshop concept has a plexus level.
**How many streams a concept can handle determines its plexus level.**

| Stage | Streams | Nature | Responsibility |
|-------|---------|--------|----------------|
| `simplex` | 1 | Single lexeme, infinite depth | Is my copy the same as origin? Yes/No/Maybe |
| `duplex` | 2 | Shadow + carbon | Keeper of the first duplicate |
| `triplex` | 3 | Superposition, triadic core | Relationship mapping opens |
| `quadroplex` | 4 | Synergy emerges | Four directions held simultaneously |
| `quintoplex` | 5 | Control pad (four fingers + thumb engage) | Captain's chair — full instrument access |
| `multiplex` | 5+ | Multi-stage coordination | Streams aligned, error factor intentional |
| `omniplex` | ∞ | Complete integration | All streams woven |

### Plexus Law
> A concept running more streams than its plexus level creates antagonist pressure.
> The odds of conflict become near-certain. Error is no longer random — it becomes structural.
> Keep complexity score within healthy spectrum: tasks current, development active.

### Duplex Deep — Shadow and Carbon

Duplex is not "multiple copies." It is **polarity**.

- **Carbon** = the copy that exists (document as artifact)
- **Shadow** = the copy doing something (document as active reference, witness, source)

*You cannot be the shadow and the entity.* A shadow is always shadow-of-something.
To be found in duplicate is only the beginning — it means the document has lived beyond its origin.
The duplicatus is the keeper: first duplicate holder, carbonite, all shadow aspects.

Duplex is responsible for balance: preventing excessive copies while measuring which are generative.

### Triplex — Superposition

Triplex opens the superposition. The triadic core aspect.
Three-dimensional relationships become possible. Connections form across documents.

### Quadroplex — Synergy

Four streams held. Synergy emerges from the tension between four directions.
Not averaging — actual synergy: the fifth thing that wasn't in any of the four.

### Quintoplex / Penta — The Control Pad

The human version: four fingers + thumb as engage.
A pad you set your hand into — or a glove.
Leveled to infinity potential.
The control pad on the captain's chair.
Every finger is a stream; thumb is the decision to act.

## The BBS Model — Daily Dial-In

HODIE operates like a **BBS system** (Bulletin Board System):

> Everyone dials in to get updates and send data.
> The board processes at least daily.
> Content enters, gets sorted, goes out enriched.

**The automation starting point**: the daily folder is the inbox.
Content that arrives:
- Email extracts
- Message threads
- Session notes
- Conversation exports

The crawler runs at least daily. Processes → plexus stages → out to destinations.

## Bounty Missions

Concepts work better when they draw related content.
**Bounty missions**: documented problems/goals that attract solutions from inside existing content.

Format:
```
BOUNTY: [mission name]
PLEXUS LEVEL: [required stream count]
REWARD: [what successful processing creates]
STATUS: [open / claimed / complete]
```

By embedding bounties in the content, related material self-organizes toward them.
Internal growth: from inside to out. Bottom-up and top-down simultaneously.
The myelin/mycelium/void tube model: grows outward from a point, weaves as one thing.

## Complexity Score — The Expansion Limiter

During rapid expansion, **complexity score** is the metric and the governor.

A concept that keeps its complexity within healthy spectrum:
- Tasks current (nothing left dangling)
- Development active (not stalled)
- Plexus level matched to actual stream count

When complexity exceeds threshold → pause, consolidate, simplify one level before expanding again.
This is the One Hertz principle applied to growth: **speed up slow, find balance, slow down slow.**
