# wellspring/

> A well flows under its own pressure — no pump required.
> Content here has been noticed, pulled out of wherever it was loose or
> tangled, and is waiting to find where it actually belongs.
> **Head pressure: 1 item at a time.**

---

## What this is

`wellspring/` is hodie's own intake/holding zone — the local well for this
sovran repo. Every repo in the unexusi ecosystem gets its own well; hodie's
is not custos's, and it is not mandelbrot's `artesian/` (the pattern this
one is modeled on). Each well flows into its own repo's destinations.

This is **not** a dump folder. Nothing sits here by default — it holds only
what was deliberately pulled out for review: things with no obvious home
yet, or things a cleanup pass flagged as ambiguous rather than guessed at.

---

## The flow

```
Loose / ambiguous content
    ↓  (noticed during cleanup, or dropped here directly)
wellspring/            ← here — waiting, reviewed one item at a time
    ↓  (eaprime1 review + disposition)
quanta/ | docs/ | mission/ | _CONSOLIDATED/ | elsewhere ← final home
```

---

## queue.md

`wellspring/queue.md` is the ordered list of what's currently waiting, and
why it landed here instead of somewhere else. When an item is reviewed and
moved to its destination (or deleted, if that's the call), it's removed
from the queue.

---

## Part of a larger network

Modeled on `mandelbrot/artesian/`'s proven pattern: "each entity/project has
its own well. Wells link to other wells." hodie's well is this one.
